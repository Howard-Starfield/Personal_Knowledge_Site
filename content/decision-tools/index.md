Good decisions leave evidence behind. A short record makes the reasoning easy
to inspect later, while a small experiment can test the assumption that carries
the most risk.

The notes in this section are original examples. They demonstrate how a second
subject area fits the Fieldbook without importing private source material.

> [!note] How to use these examples
> Pick one decision that is still reversible. Write down the evidence that
> would change your choice before you ask other people for opinions.
