This course takes a learner who is comfortable in the terminal and already uses LLMs daily, and builds toward AI systems that are guarded, evaluated, and deployable for real business problems. Each chapter follows a dependency order: AI and LLM basics, prompting and structured output, embeddings and search, RAG, memory, agents and tools, guardrails and security, evals, model customization, and finally production and career packaging.

You do not need a formal computer science background. You do need time to build rather than only read, because every chapter ends in something you can run and show.

## How this course works

- Each lesson teaches one chapter as a sequence of small steps, with a plain-language explanation, a concrete example, a precise rule, and a worked example in each.
- A retrieval checkpoint follows each step or pair of steps, so you practice recalling the idea before you move on.
- An end-of-chapter quiz revisits the questions the checkpoints did not use.
- An "Explain it back" prompt asks you to restate the whole lesson in your own words.
