    prompt: "A colleague says CalHEERS is just the online form that consumers fill in. Explain what is wrong with that, and list what CalHEERS does beyond the form."
    sampleAnswer: "CalHEERS is the California Health Care Eligibility, Enrollment and Retention System, and it is the whole system behind the form rather than the form itself. Beyond what a consumer types in, it gathers the consumer's data, works out household eligibility, supports plan selection and refers the consumer on to the health plan's own external site, takes payments, carries out ongoing system updates, sends messages to consumers, and issues the forms the programme is required to send. The training also expects the name in full, with Health Care as two words."
    explanation: "A complete answer does three things: it gives the name in full, it says the form is only the part a consumer sees, and it names the other functions - data collection, eligibility determination, plan selection and referral to the carrier's own site, payments, system updates, consumer communications and the required forms. An answer that only says it is more than a form has not told you what you are looking at. Lesson section: Stage 1: CalHEERS is the whole system, not just the form"

    prompt: "You are about to search for a consumer's case. Explain what the two search methods take, which one will already be selected, and what you should check before you press search."
    sampleAnswer: "There are two radio buttons under Search by. Consumer information takes the consumer's name, date of birth, social security number, or the email address on their account. Unique identifier takes the CalHEERS case ID, the application ID, or the username attached to the case. The selection defaults to whichever search type was used most recently, so it does not reset between calls: if the last search was by case ID, unique identifier is still selected. Before searching you look at which button is selected, and you know that a combo search on last name, first name and date of birth returns every case attached to that consumer's contact."
    explanation: "A complete answer gives both methods and their fields, says the button carries over from the last search rather than resetting, and adds the combo search that returns every case for the contact. An answer that lists the fields but not the carry-over leaves the learner with no reason to look at the screen before typing. Lesson section: Stage 4: The search method you used last time is the one you get"
