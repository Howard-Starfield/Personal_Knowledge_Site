    sampleAnswer: "No documents travel with the application, because the details are checked against government records and against information the consumer has already given; if something further is needed, Covered California contacts the consumer and names the specific documents it wants. Two rules govern the content: only what the consumer answered goes on the form, and the consumer has to be honest on the application, because those answers are what the shopping tool and the application both work from."

Lesson section: Step 3: What goes into the application."
