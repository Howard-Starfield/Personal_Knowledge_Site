    type: free-recall
    prompt: "A consumer asks you to explain what a premium, a copayment, coinsurance and a deductible each are. Set out the four in plain words."
    sampleAnswer: "The premium is the amount paid each month to keep a plan in force. A copayment is a flat dollar figure paid for a covered service, such as a fixed charge for a primary care visit. Coinsurance is the percentage paid on a covered bill once the deductible has been met. The deductible is what a member pays out of pocket before the plan starts sharing the cost, and it also counts towards the out-of-pocket maximum. The training noted that the monthly payment, the percentage and the out-of-pocket figure are the three answers that get mistaken for a copayment."
    explanation: "A complete answer names all four terms and says what each one is: the monthly premium, the flat dollar copayment, the percentage coinsurance, and the deductible as the amount paid before the plan shares cost and part of the out-of-pocket maximum. An answer that only separates the premium from the copayment is incomplete, and one that describes coinsurance as a fixed dollar figure has the wrong one. Lesson section: Step 1: The words for money."
