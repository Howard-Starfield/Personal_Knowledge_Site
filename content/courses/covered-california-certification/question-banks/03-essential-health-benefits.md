    prompt: "Name all ten essential health benefit categories that every qualifying plan must cover."
    sampleAnswer: "The ten are: 1) ambulatory patient services; 2) emergency services; 3) hospitalization; 4) maternity and newborn care; 5) mental health and substance use disorder services, including behavioural health treatment; 6) prescription drugs; 7) rehabilitative and habilitative services and devices; 8) laboratory services; 9) preventive and wellness services and chronic disease management; 10) paediatric services, including oral and vision care. It is a complete list of ten rather than a sample, and it applies to non-grandfathered plans in both the individual and the small-group markets."
    explanation: "A correct answer gives all ten. Watch the three that get dropped or reshaped most often: behavioural health treatment belongs inside the mental health and substance use disorder category rather than standing alone, the rehabilitative and habilitative category covers services and devices, and the paediatric category explicitly includes oral and vision care. An answer with eight or nine categories has not given the closed list of ten. Lesson section: Step 2: The ten benefit categories."

    prompt: "Explain which plans the requirement to cover essential health benefits reaches, and what that requirement does and does not tell you about what a member pays."
    sampleAnswer: "The requirement reaches non-grandfathered plans in both the individual and the small-group markets, so two qualifiers matter: the plans it covers are the non-grandfathered ones, and the two markets it applies to are individual and small-group. What the requirement does is set a floor on what a plan has to offer. What it does not do is say anything about the member's share of the cost, because deductibles, copayments and out-of-pocket maximums are set by the plan and differ between categories."
    explanation: "Look for both qualifiers, non-grandfathered and the pair of markets, and for the separation between what must be covered and what the member pays. An answer that says every plan must cover the ten has dropped the first qualifier, and an answer that implies the requirement fixes the cost of care has collapsed the two things the lesson keeps apart. Lesson section: Step 1: What the law requires, and of which plans."
