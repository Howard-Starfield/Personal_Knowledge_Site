    prompt: "What are the two systems that can tell different stories about a household's coverage, and what does each one hold?"
    sampleAnswer: "The Covered California enrolment system and the carrier's own system each hold a record of the same household's coverage, and they do not always agree. The enrolment system holds the application, the eligibility determination, the plan selection and the first payment, along with a record of what was changed and when. The carrier's system holds the policies the insurer has issued and the coverage the carrier believes is active. The two feed each other and the feed is not instant, so a change made in the enrolment system has to travel to the carrier before the carrier's own screen changes. The consumer is the one who sees the gap, because they have been given two answers by two organisations about whether they are covered."
    explanation: "Look for both systems, what each one owns, and the fact that the consumer is the one who notices the disagreement. An answer that treats one system as simply the correct one, or that leaves out why the consumer is caught between them, has missed the point of the lesson. Lesson section: Step 1: Two systems, two records."
