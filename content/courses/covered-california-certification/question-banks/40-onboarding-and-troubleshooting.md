    type: free-recall
    prompt: "Someone says their camera is greyed out in the meeting. What do you work through, in what order?"
    sampleAnswer: "Work the ladder in order. First the computer's own settings: allow the camera and the microphone, and check the user name on the account. Then the join options: when you click to join, does it offer to show your camera, and use the new link you were sent. Then unplug the device and plug it back in. Then clear the cookies. Then restart the whole machine, after the cookies rather than before. Start at the bottom and work up; do not open at the restart."
    explanation: "Look for the five rungs in order and for the cookies before the restart. An answer that puts the restart first has skipped the ladder, and an answer that leaves the join options out has missed the step that tells you whether the camera is reaching the meeting at all. Lesson section: Step 2: The ladder of fixes you can hand over."
  - id: "14cdce95-2140-475f-9c01-0033fd475e5a"
    revision: 1
    status: active
    type: free-recall
    prompt: "Someone is blocked and a deadline of theirs is tomorrow. What has to be said, and what has to be found?"
    sampleAnswer: "Two things. First, sort the deadline into one of two kinds and say which one it is out loud — a requirement, or a reminder. In the session it was a reminder, and the person was told so plainly. Second, find the fallback that leaves them whole while the blocker is worked on: they were still going to be paid by cheque whether or not the direct payment was ever set up. And the real fix still goes on the ticket, because a workaround and a fix are not alternatives. Someone who is told a deadline is a requirement and that it will be sorted out later has been handed two problems instead of one."
    explanation: "Look for the requirement against reminder distinction, the fallback, and the fact that the ticket still goes in. An answer that only reassures without naming a fallback has not removed the cost, and an answer that treats the workaround as a substitute for the fix has left the blocker where it was. Lesson section: Step 4: Do not let a blocked task cost the person something."
