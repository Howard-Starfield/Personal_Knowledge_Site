    prompt: "A caller cannot give you the answers that authenticate them. Explain what you must not do, what you may do instead, and what you say to the caller."
    sampleAnswer: "On a failed authentication you release nothing: no case information, no account or case changes, no login details and no password reset. What you do instead is ask for the details as they appear on the caller's application or on other official documents, and check whether an official document matches what they gave you. If the caller can be authenticated that way, offer to correct the application so that it matches the documents. If it does not match, say plainly that the information they have given is not enough to authenticate them, and offer to help with general questions. You can also point them at coveredca.com so that they can sign in and correct their own details, and suggest that the primary contact calls the service centre on 800-300-1506 and adds the caller as an authorised representative. What the caller is told is that the details they supplied are not enough to authenticate them, not that there is no case and not that they have done anything wrong."
    explanation: "A complete answer gives the four things that are barred once authentication has failed, the route through the official documents, the offer to correct the application, and the wording of what the caller is told. An answer that only says you cannot release information has stopped one step short, because the correction offer and the offer of general help are part of what the caller is entitled to.     prompt: "A caller is not the primary contact on the case. Explain whose information they have to give to be authenticated, and what you check about their relationship before the call goes any further."
    sampleAnswer: "They give the primary contact's legal first and last name, and every answer they give is about the primary rather than about themselves. Knowing the primary's details is what authenticates the call; the role the caller holds is what decides what you may then do. The primary does not have to be on the line at all. What you check next is the relationship, and how depends on the role: a spouse or domestic partner is checked on the Review Application page in the Individual Information tab, where the primary's marital status and the household relationship have to agree with the consumer's own; an authorised representative is found on the account's authorised representative page; and a delegate is found on the Manage Delegates page. Household means the tax household, so a partner who is not on the same tax return is a non-primary adult enrollee and gets the narrower set of rights."
    explanation: "A complete answer separates the two jobs, proving knowledge of the primary and establishing the caller's standing, and names where each relationship is checked. An answer that stops at the primary's name has not covered the second check at all, and an answer that treats the caller as the primary once they know the details misses the point of the role table. Lesson section: Stage 3: Who is on the line"

    type: free-recall
    prompt: "Explain the authentication standard: what the caller has to produce, how many separate items it adds up to, which record the answers are checked against, and what an email item does when the case has no email address on it."
    sampleAnswer: "The caller produces the primary's legal first and last name, which counts as one item, plus three correct answers from the approved list about the primary: date of birth, full mailing or physical address, phone number, the final four digits of the social security number, the email address, or the names of every household member recorded on the case. That is four items in total. The training gave it as the name and then at least three further items, with the date of birth, the phone number and the mailing address the preferred ones, and also as the primary's first and last name, their date of birth and at least two further pieces; the follow-up spelled out that three plus the name is four. The answers are checked in the Case Overview Hub, reached from the ellipsis under Action in Case Overview, and the application PDF in the Application History page is not used because it may not reflect the latest information. If the case has no email address on it and the caller tells you that no email address is held on the account, that item is authenticated; an address that is not on the account is not a pass."
    explanation: "A complete answer gives the approved list, the count of four items, the Case Overview Hub, the reason the application PDF is rejected, and both halves of the blank-email rule. An answer that says three without saying that the name is a fourth item has half the standard, and an answer that validates against the PDF has the source of truth wrong. Lesson section: Stage 4: The authentication standard"
