    sampleAnswer: "An appeal is a request to review a coverage decision, and the consumer is the one who submits it.     sampleAnswer: "Covered California screens and the county decides. The role of Covered California is to determine whether an applicant may be Medi-Cal eligible and then to pass that information to the county of the applicant's residence through the online application, and the county makes the determination. Every consumer who completes a subsidised Covered California application is automatically reviewed for Medi-Cal eligibility, so it is not one programme or the other. On a case that turns out to be a Medi-Cal matter the consumer goes to their local county office, and you do not report a change on a Medi-Cal case, you do not add a plan, you do not cancel a plan and you do not reset the consumer's password. You still authenticate the consumer as usual and you still document the call as usual."
    explanation: "Look for who decides and who screens, for the automatic review of every subsidised applicant, and for the four changes that belong to the county. An answer that says Covered California approves Medi-Cal eligibility has swapped the two sides of the boundary, and an answer that omits the four county changes has left the practical rule out. Lesson section: Stage 4: Where the Medi-Cal line sits."
