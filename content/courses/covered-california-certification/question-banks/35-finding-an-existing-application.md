    prompt: "A consumer's application cannot be found through the search. Which field on the case controls that, and what do you do about it every time you work on an existing application?"
    sampleAnswer: "The CalHEERS case identifier on the case controls it. The training said that without that field the record cannot be retrieved through the search function, and that the identifier sits on the case summary at the end of the record. So every time you touch an existing application you copy the identifier into a note on the case, which is what the training described as putting everything into the case so that the next person can find it. There is also no view that lets you search by that number, so the identifier is something you record rather than something you look up by."
    explanation: "Look for the identifier, for the note, and for the reason the note matters. An answer that says the consumer has no application, or that suggests searching by the CalHEERS number itself, has missed the point of the field. Lesson section: Step 3: When the record cannot be found."

    prompt: "What four facts does the income-change flow need before it will accept a new amount?"
    sampleAnswer: "It needs the new amount, the main reason for the change, the frequency the income is reported in, and the date the new amount started. In the training's worked example the amount went from $45,000 a year up to $60,000 a year, the reason was a change of amount, the frequency was annual, and the new amount began on 1 October 2026."
    explanation: "Look for all four facts, the four reason codes, and the three frequencies. An answer that gives the amount and the date but no reason and no frequency has left out half of what the flow asks for. Lesson section: Step 5: The four inputs to an income change."

    prompt: "A consumer's annual income on the application was $45,000, and it becomes $60,000 from 1 October 2026. What yearly figure does the record then show, and why is it not $60,000?"
    sampleAnswer: "The record shows a yearly household income of $48,750, next to a current money income of about $5,000 a month. It is not $60,000 because the new amount only applies from October. The consumer asked whether the difference was tax coming out, and the training's answer was that it is an average: the year ran at $45,000 from January to September and at $60,000 from October, which blends to $48,750. The amount you enter is what the consumer earns now; the yearly figure is worked out for you from the amount and the start date."
    explanation: "Look for $48,750, for the part-year explanation, and for the rejection of the tax explanation. An answer that says $60,000, or that explains the difference as tax being taken out, has repeated the misunderstanding the training corrected. Lesson section: Step 6: Why the yearly figure is $48,750."
