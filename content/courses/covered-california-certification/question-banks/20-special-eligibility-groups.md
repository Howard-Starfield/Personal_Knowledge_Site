    prompt: "Explain when a young adult loses the right to stay on a parent's health plan as a dependent, and what happens on the 26th birthday."
    sampleAnswer: "The right to stay as a dependent runs to 26, and turning 26 does not cancel cover on the birthday or in that month. A dependent whose 26th birthday falls during a benefit year stays on the plan until the end of that year, so a 26th birthday on 9 October 2026 means cover runs through 31 December 2026. Marriage, having a child, leaving home, starting work, filing taxes on your own or moving out do not end it before 26. To stay beyond 26 the person must meet both of two conditions: being incapable of self-sustaining employment because of a physical or mental disability, injury, illness or condition, and being chiefly dependent on the subscriber for support and maintenance."
    explanation: "A correct answer says the age-26 rule ends cover at the end of the benefit year rather than on the birthday, gives 31 December 2026 as the end date for a 2026 birthday, lists the events that do not end cover before 26, and gives both of the conditions for staying beyond 26. An answer that says the birthday cancels the plan is wrong. An answer that names only one of the two conditions for staying past 26 is wrong, because the contract language requires both.     prompt: "What is minimum essential coverage, and how does it apply to students, veterans, and people with Medicare?"
    sampleAnswer: "Minimum essential coverage is cover that satisfies the requirement to have health insurance, and a consumer who has it is not eligible for financial assistance through Covered California while they have it. A student whose school health plan carries the minimum essential coverage designation is not eligible for tax credits through Covered California while covered by that plan, so the student should check with the college about whether the plan carries it. All Veterans Affairs health care programmes meet the requirement, and a veteran enrolled in one may terminate their VA cover and enrol in a Covered California plan with financial assistance if eligible, during the open enrolment period. A consumer who has Medicare Part A, or who has chosen a Medicare Advantage plan, also meets the requirement, while Medicare Part B on its own does not."
