    prompt: "What do children under 19 have for dental and vision, and does any financial help attach to it?"
    sampleAnswer: "Children under 19 get dental and vision inside the health plan they hold through Covered California, at no extra cost. For dental, cleanings, check-ups and X-rays are free, and the member splits the cost of fillings, of root canals and of crowns with the dental company, up to $350 where there is one child, or $700 where there are two or more. For vision, the cover includes eye check-ups and one pair of glasses a year, or contact lenses instead, and no deductible applies to children's vision benefits. No financial assistance and no premium tax credit attaches to dental or vision whatever the household earns, and a family that declines the embedded cover may buy separate cover instead, with its own premium, its own deductible and its own out-of-pocket maximum."
    explanation: "Look for the children's age, the no-extra-cost framing, the two children's cost-share figures and the vision benefits, and above all the rule that no financial help attaches to dental or vision. An answer that gives children their own dental premium, or that leaves the absence of financial help out, has missed the point the training tested twice. Lesson section: Step 2: Children's dental and vision come with the plan."
