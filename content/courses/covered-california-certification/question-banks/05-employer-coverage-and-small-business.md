    prompt: "In your own words, state the employer shared responsibility rule: who it applies to, what the employer has to offer, and what happens if it does not do that."
    sampleAnswer: "An employer with 50 or more full-time equivalent employees has to offer health coverage that is both affordable and provides minimum value to its full-time employees and to their dependent children under 26. If the employer does not offer that coverage, it pays the employer shared responsibility penalty instead. A business with fewer than 50 full-time equivalents is not subject to the rule at all. A full-time equivalent is a calculation rather than a plain headcount, because part-time hours are folded in."
    explanation: "A good answer names the threshold, both conditions on the coverage, who the coverage has to reach, and the penalty as the alternative. An answer that stops at the threshold, or that describes the penalty as a possibility rather than the alternative, has left half of the rule out. Lesson section: Step 1: The employer mandate."
