    prompt: "A consumer calls because the marketplace has asked them for a document it could not check for itself. Explain what the reasonable opportunity period is, what the shorthand ROP stands for, and what happens to the consumer's coverage while the period is running."
    sampleAnswer: "The reasonable opportunity period is the window a consumer gets to send a document themselves when the marketplace cannot verify something electronically, such as income or an immigration status. ROP is the short form the service centre uses for it, and the same shorthand is also used for a termination that came about because the period ran out with nothing uploaded. The consumer is not made ineligible while the period runs: the training is explicit that the consumer stays eligible until the documents are in, so the coverage continues while the window is open. The period therefore protects the consumer rather than penalising them, and the two things the consumer needs from a representative are the date the document is due and what happens if that date passes."
    explanation: "A complete answer defines the period as the window in which the consumer uploads what the system could not verify, says that ROP is the short form the service centre uses for it, and states that the consumer stays eligible until the documents are in. The part usually missing is that the same shorthand is also used for the termination that follows, and that the consumer's cover continues throughout. An answer that treats the period as a penalty, or that leaves the consumer unsure whether they are still covered, does not answer the question.
