    prompt: "Explain the difference between the standard consumer view and the flexible administrator view of the application, say which one you use first, and say what a consumer cannot do in the other one."
    sampleAnswer: "The standard consumer view shows the application page by page, one section at a time. The flexible administrator view puts the whole application on one continuous screen. You select the standard consumer view first, every time, and treat the flexible view as the backup for the call where the standard one is not working. A consumer cannot view or edit the application in the flexible administrator view, so it is never somewhere you can send a consumer to make a change themselves - their own account shows the standard, page-by-page application."
    explanation: "A complete answer states both layouts, puts the standard consumer view first as a rule rather than a preference, calls the flexible view a backup, and states that a consumer cannot view or edit the application there. An answer that describes both views without the ordering rule leaves the learner to guess which one to open. Lesson section: Stage 4: Two views of the consumer application"

    prompt: "A consumer has already submitted an application, agreed to the disclaimers, and does not like the eligibility results. Explain what you do, and say what happens to the original submission."
    sampleAnswer: "You go back and edit the application, and the consumer has to hear the disclaimers again, because agreeing to the first version is not consent to the corrected one.
