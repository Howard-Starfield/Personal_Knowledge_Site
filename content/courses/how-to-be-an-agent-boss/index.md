## Why Become an "Agent Boss"?

Most professionals today use AI as a high-speed search engine or a clever autocomplete. But the frontier has shifted: **we are no longer just prompting language models—we are hiring, onboarding, delegating to, and managing autonomous digital teammates.**

An AI Agent is not a passive document summarizer. It has goals, tool connections, organizational memory, and the autonomy to act in business software on your behalf. Managing an agent portfolio requires the exact same discipline as managing human teams—except agents do not sleep, their compute costs can compound exponentially in seconds, and when their instructions drift, they can fail with total confidence.

This course teaches you how to step into the role of an **Agent Boss**: the leader who knows how to spot genuine high-ROI agent opportunities, define crystal-clear operational boundaries, monitor health metrics, optimize compute spend, and guide digital agents across their entire lifecycle.
