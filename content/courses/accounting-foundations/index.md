## Why Accounting Is the Language of Business

Warren Buffett famously observed that **accounting is the language of business**. If you cannot speak and interpret the language, you cannot navigate the territory—you are left making strategic decisions blindfolded, relying on secondhand intuition while those who understand the numbers command the capital.

Yet traditional accounting education often obscures intuitive reality behind a dense fog of memorized debits, credits, and ledger mechanics. Students memorize mechanical rules without grasping what the numbers actually represent: **the economic pulse, physical resources, legal obligations, and cash flows of an enterprise.**

This course strips away the bureaucratic clutter. Built on the **Feynman Learning Technique**, each unit breaks down financial and managerial concepts from first principles using vivid real-world analogies:

- The **Scorekeeper vs. The Game** (understanding why accounting is a recording mechanism, not reality itself).
- The **Photographic Snapshot vs. The Video Reel** (contrasting the point-in-time Balance Sheet with period-over-period Income and Cash Flow statements).
- The **DuPont Engine** (dissecting how profit margin, asset turn, and financial leverage multiply to create return on equity).
- The **Corporate Plumbing System** (tracking why a business can be wildly profitable on paper while suffocating from a lack of cash liquidity).

Whether you are an engineer designing software, an executive managing a P&L, an entrepreneur launching a venture, or an investor allocating capital, this course equips you with the mental models and quantitative tools to read, analyze, and leverage accounting information with total confidence.
