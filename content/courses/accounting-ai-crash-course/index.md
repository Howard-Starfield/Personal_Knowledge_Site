This course is a practical accounting refresher for someone with a business or accounting background who wants to work at the accounts payable, accounts receivable, junior staff or bookkeeper level. Every lesson is built around one fictional company, BoBo Digital Services LLC, so the examples build on each other. All figures are sample data and personal study material, not paid client work.

The course moves from foundations (why accounting exists, the accounting equation, debits and credits, the chart of accounts) through everyday bookkeeping and month-end close, then financial statements, controls and audit evidence, and finally data tools and AI-assisted workflows with explicit guardrails.

## How this course works

- Each lesson teaches one chapter in small, step-by-step stages, with a plain-language explanation, an analogy, the precise rule and a worked example.
- After each step or two you get a retrieval checkpoint: a short quiz that makes you recall what you just learned instead of rereading it.
- Each lesson ends with an end-of-chapter quiz covering the questions the checkpoints did not use.
- Every lesson asks you to "Explain it back" in your own words, which is the strongest way to find gaps.
