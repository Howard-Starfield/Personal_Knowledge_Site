This compact course turns the retrieval decision chain into a repeatable working
loop. It uses original demonstration material and does not require an account,
stored progress, or a completion gate.

## Recommended route

Begin with [[courses/retrieval-field-guide/lessons/01-frame-the-question|Frame the question]].

Then use [[courses/retrieval-field-guide/lessons/02-design-the-evidence-loop|Design the evidence loop]] to decide what to measure and what to change.

| Lesson | Purpose                                                        | Estimated time |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------: |
| 1      | Define the claim, evidence, and boundary before searching.     |      8 minutes |
| 2      | Evaluate evidence selection and answer use as separate stages. |     10 minutes |
