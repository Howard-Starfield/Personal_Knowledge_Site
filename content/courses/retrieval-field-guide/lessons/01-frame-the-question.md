A search query is not yet an evidence need. Before retrieving, write down the
claim you expect to support, the evidence that could change your mind, and the
constraints that make one source useful for this decision.

## The framing card

1. **Decision:** What will you do differently after learning the answer?
2. **Claim:** What statement needs support or challenge?
3. **Evidence:** What observation or source unit would be sufficient?
4. **Constraints:** Which date, audience, jurisdiction, version, or context
   changes relevance?
5. **Stop condition:** What would make the evidence inadequate?

> [!question] Check your framing
> Could two different answers both be correct because the question omitted a
> version, audience, or time boundary?
