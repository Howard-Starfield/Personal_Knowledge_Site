## Why Account Management Matters

In high-growth organizations, commercial culture disproportionately glorifies the **closing of the new deal**. Sales bells ring, commission checks are celebrated, and executive dashboards fixate on new customer acquisition.

Yet modern business arithmetic tells an uncompromisingly different story:

- **Acquiring a new customer costs 5 to 7 times more** than retaining an existing account.
- Increasing customer retention rates by a mere **5% can expand operating profits by 25% to 95%** due to operating leverage, cross-sell velocity, and reduced support friction.
- Every lost client represents not merely lost monthly subscription or contract revenue, but the catastrophic write-off of customer acquisition cost (CAC) and the destruction of downstream referral networks.

Closing a deal is not the finish line—it is the ceremonial ribbon-cutting of a long-term enterprise partnership. True enterprise value compounds in the **post-sale lifecycle**: the weeks, quarters, and years where promises made during the sales cycle are audited against delivered operational reality.

This course reconstructs post-sale relationship management from first principles. Rooted in the **Feynman Learning Technique**, each unit strips away platitudes to provide concrete mental models, actionable behavioral frameworks, quantitative economics, and defensive operational playbooks.
