The Knowledge Fieldbook is a public reading and practice space built from
Markdown. Articles explain; courses sequence; annotations expose unfinished
thinking; the Learning Log keeps follow-ups discoverable.

## Editorial rules readers can rely on

- A published page remains public without a login.
- The page's identity survives a rename or move.
- Internal links written into the prose express intentional relationships.
- Explorer links, generated listings, tags, and backlinks aid navigation but do
  not silently claim the same relationship.
- Questions, answers, and explanations are public learning material—not secret
  assessment data.

## Current maturity

This local showcase proves the static Quartz reading experience. Strict content
validation, final category/course indexes, and the executable bank-backed quiz
system remain implementation work. Owner capture and synchronized state are
later phases.
