Every lesson in this fieldbook that carries a quiz links its own answer key,
so a lesson can be finished and its reasoning checked without hunting through
a course. This page gathers the assessment material in one place.

The answers are public. Each answer key shows the reasoning behind every
answer, and asks the same questions the lesson asks. Nothing here is a
secure or proctored test.
