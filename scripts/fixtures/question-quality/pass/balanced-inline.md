### Question 1

Which unit measures electrical resistance?

- A) The ohm
- B) The volt
- C) The ampere
- D) The watt

<details class="quiz-answer">
<summary>Reveal Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Correct Answer:** A

Resistance is measured in ohms.

- A) is correct because the ohm is the unit of resistance.
- B) is wrong because the volt measures potential difference.

</details>
